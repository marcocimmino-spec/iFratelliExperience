The Full-Day Experience

From your hotel door, and back again

A single price, a whole day, zero stress. We handle the driving, the tickets, and the food — you just enjoy.

We Pick You Up

Comfortable private pick-up directly from your hotel in Positano. No queues, no public buses.

2

We drive you to the volcano and walk you up to the crater — the bay of Naples spread out below.

4

Back to Your Hotel

Full and happy, we bring you straight back to Positano. The whole day taken care of.

Who We Are

Two brothers, born and raised by the bay

We are two young Neapolitans who grew up in this world. Our family ran a restaurant for as long as we can remember, so hospitality and good food are simply in our blood.

We've travelled the world, met people from every corner of it, and always came back to the same thought: there is no place like home. I Fratelli del Vesuvio is our way of sharing the real Naples — the volcano, the views, and a pizza made the way our family always made it. Not a tourist factory, just two friends showing you our home.

Meet the Brothers

Marco Cimmino

Host & Storyteller

Ciro Secondulfo

Tour Leader

Also the son of a restaurant owner, Ciro currently works as a receptionist at Hotel Le Felci in Fiuggi and is a tour guide — he knows how to make every day run smoothly.

Not brothers by blood, but brothers all the same — we grew up next door to each other and have been best friends our whole lives.
